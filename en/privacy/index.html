1. Data protection at a glance

General information

The following notes give a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data that can be used to identify you personally. Detailed information on data protection can be found in this privacy policy.

Data collection on this website

Who is responsible for data collection on this website?
Data on this website is processed by the website operator. Their contact details can be found in the section “Information about the controller” in this privacy policy.

How do I collect your data?
Firstly, your data is collected when you give it to me, for example in an email. Secondly, technical connection data arises when the website is opened (e.g. IP address, browser, time of the request). This is collected by the host, see the section “Hosting”. I do not collect any data about your visit myself.

What do I use your data for?
I use data from an email to answer your enquiry. This website uses no analytics or tracking tools and does not analyse your behaviour.

What rights do you have regarding your data?
You have the right at any time to receive information free of charge about the origin, recipients and purpose of your stored personal data. You also have the right to request its rectification or erasure, and, under certain circumstances, the restriction of its processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. You can contact me at any time about this or any other data protection question.

2. Hosting

GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a Microsoft Corporation company), “GitHub” below.

When you open this website, GitHub records your IP address and stores it for security purposes, whether or not you are signed in to GitHub. Technically necessary connection data such as the time of the request, the file requested, the referrer and browser and operating system information (user agent) may also be recorded. GitHub decides how long it keeps this data; I have no influence on that. I do not receive the access data and do not combine it with other data.

The processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is the secure, fast and efficient provision of this website by a professional provider.

Your IP address may be transferred to the United States in the process. GitHub states that it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which ensures an adequate level of protection for certified companies. Details on GitHub's processing are in GitHub's privacy statement.

General information on the legal bases of processing

Where your data is needed to perform a contract or take pre-contractual steps, I process it under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. I also process it where necessary to comply with a legal obligation, under Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. Processing may further be based on my legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I state the legal basis that applies in each case in the following paragraphs. I do not ask for consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) on this website because no processing requiring consent takes place.

Recipients of personal data

I only pass personal data to external parties where this is necessary to perform a contract, where I am legally obliged to (e.g. to tax authorities), where I have a legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or where another legal basis permits it. The recipient when you open this website is the host, GitHub (see “Hosting”).

Right to object in special cases and to direct marketing (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where data processing is based on Art. 6(1)(e) or (f) GDPR, you have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of your personal data; this also applies to profiling based on these provisions. The legal basis on which processing rests can be found in this privacy policy. If you object, I will no longer process the personal data concerned unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims (objection under Art. 21(1) GDPR).

Where your personal data is processed for direct marketing, you have the right to object at any time to the processing of personal data concerning you for such marketing; this also applies to profiling insofar as it is related to such direct marketing. If you object, your personal data will no longer be used for direct marketing (objection under Art. 21(2) GDPR).

Right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority

In the event of GDPR infringements, data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of their habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. This right exists without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy. The authority responsible for me is the Saxon Data Protection Commissioner (Sächsische Datenschutzbeauftragte), Devrientstraße 5, 01067 Dresden, Germany.

Right to data portability

You have the right to have data that I process automatically on the basis of your consent or to perform a contract handed to you or to a third party in a common, machine-readable format. If you ask for the data to be transferred directly to another controller, this will only be done where technically feasible.

Access, rectification and erasure

Within the scope of the applicable statutory provisions, you have the right at any time to free information about your stored personal data, its origin and recipients and the purpose of the processing and, where applicable, a right to rectification or erasure. You can contact me at any time about this or any other question on personal data.

Right to restriction of processing

You have the right to request the restriction of the processing of your personal data. You can contact me at any time about this. The right to restriction exists in the following cases:

  • If you contest the accuracy of your personal data stored with me, I usually need time to check this. For the duration of the check you have the right to request restriction of processing.
  • If the processing of your personal data was or is unlawful, you can request restriction instead of erasure.
  • If I no longer need your personal data but you need it to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, you have the right to request restriction instead of erasure.
  • If you have objected under Art. 21(1) GDPR, your interests and mine have to be weighed. As long as it is not yet clear whose interests prevail, you have the right to request restriction of processing.

If you have restricted the processing of your personal data, this data may — apart from being stored — only be processed with your consent or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, to protect the rights of another natural or legal person, or for reasons of important public interest of the European Union or a member state.

No automated decision-making

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

TLS encryption

For security reasons this site uses TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the browser's address bar changing from “http://” to “https://” and by the padlock symbol in your browser bar. When encryption is active, the data you send to me cannot be read by third parties.

4. Data collection on this website

No cookies, no tracking, no third parties

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (no cookies, local storage or similar). It embeds nothing from other servers and uses no analytics or tracking services. Fonts and images load only from this domain. No cookie banner is therefore required.

The page runs a small piece of JavaScript that only assembles my email address into a link, to protect it from automated harvesting. It collects and stores no data and sends nothing to a server.

Enquiries by email

If you contact me by email, your enquiry including all personal data arising from it (name, email address, enquiry) is stored and processed by me to handle your request. I do not pass this data on without your consent.

This data is processed under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to the performance of a contract or is necessary to take pre-contractual steps. In all other cases the processing is based on my legitimate interest in effectively handling the enquiries addressed to me (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The data you send me by email stays with me until you ask me to delete it or the purpose of storage no longer applies (e.g. after your request has been dealt with). Mandatory statutory provisions — in particular statutory retention periods — remain unaffected.

Links to LinkedIn and Instagram

On the homepage I link to my profiles on LinkedIn and Instagram. These are plain links, not embedded content: no data is transmitted to these providers when this website is opened. Only when you click one of the links do you leave this website, and the provider's privacy terms apply.
