Johann Schumacher

I'm a UI/UX designer and frontend developer based in Radebeul near Dresden, working on projects from the first sketch through to shipped code. I'm drawn to interfaces for unusual contexts, code that's still readable in five years, and record sleeves from the seventies. My day job is at GTV, where I build software for museums, exhibitions and industry. Alongside that I take on freelance web and app work, and currently have capacity for new projects.
